# Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the game.

To delete your account:

1. Open the game.
2. Go to the Profile button.
3. Select "Delete Account".
4. Confirm the deletion.

Once confirmed, all data associated with your account stored on our servers will be permanently deleted, including your game progress and profile information.

Please note that this action cannot be undone.

Your Google Play Games account will not be deleted, as it is managed by Google. Only the data stored by our game service will be removed.

If you are unable to access the game and need assistance with account deletion, please contact our support team classbitgames@gmail.com
