Neotryx ("the Game") is developed and operated by Crismcollins. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.
By playing Neotryx, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect
Information from Google Play Games:

Display name
Profile avatar
Google Play player ID

Game progress data:

Level reached

Automatically collected data:

Device type and operating system
App usage and performance data (crashes, errors)
Advertising identifier (GAID)
Approximate location (country/region, derived from IP)


3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data to:

Authenticate your identity via Google Play Games
Display your profile and ranking on the leaderboard
Save and sync your game progress
Send push notifications about game updates and offers
Show personalized and non-personalized ads
Analyze app performance and fix crashes
Process in-app purchases

5. Advertising
Neotryx displays ads through LevelPlay mediation (AdMob, Unity Ads, IronSource). These providers may use your advertising identifier to show personalized ads.
You can opt out of personalized ads at any time:

Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID


6. Push Notifications
We use OneSignal to send push notifications. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

7. In-App Purchases
Neotryx offers a "Remove Ads" purchase processed through the Google Play Store. We do not store payment information — all transactions are handled by Google Play.

8. Data Retention
We retain your game data (name, avatar, level) as long as your account is active on our leaderboard. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy
Neotryx is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights
Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

Request correction or deletion of your data
Opt out of personalized advertising
Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
